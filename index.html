The Founding Insight
The data already exists. No one has captured it.
Core Thesis
Every precision-oncology and image-guided procedure generates 80–200 structured data fields at the moment it executes — device logs, DICOM RT objects, RS-232 streams, MRI thermometry. Those fields are never extracted, never linked to outcomes, never made available for research or drug development. PROCEDA captures them automatically, links them to patient outcomes via standard HL7, and aggregates them into the only procedure-physics registry ever built at multi-site scale.

The gap that makes PROCEDA possible: EHRs capture what was ordered. Procedure notes capture what the physician wrote down. PROCEDA captures what the device actually delivered — and links it directly to what happened to the patient afterward. That gap between ordered and delivered is where pharmaceutical-grade insight lives.
Why it hasn't been done before: Building procedure-level capture across 6 different modalities requires physics-layer expertise in each. Radiation oncology, robotic surgery, and thermal ablation have no shared data standards. No EHR vendor has the clinical depth to build it. No device company has the incentive to share across competitors. PROCEDA sits at the only intersection where this is possible: independent, multi-device, federated by design.
Why IORT First
  • Fastest physics-to-outcome signal: Single-fraction intraoperative treatment with 6–24 month outcome window. No fractionation complexity. Signal clarity is highest per case.
  • No existing registry: Zero multi-site IORT procedure-physics registries exist globally. PROCEDA is the only source of this data.
  • Device homogeneity: Zeiss Intrabeam and Sensus SRT both output RS-232 streams with identical field maps. Parser written once, deployed everywhere.
  • Proof-of-concept velocity: 84 automated fields, 97% completeness, operational in 5 countries. Validates the infrastructure thesis before expanding to higher-complexity verticals.
  • Regulatory pathway clarity: IORT data does not trigger IDE or PMA requirements. Registry data collection is IRB-approved observational research at all sites.

Federated Architecture
Raw device data never leaves the institution's network. Only structured extracted fields are transmitted to the central PROCEDA registry. HIPAA-compliant by architectural design — not a compliance overlay bolted on afterward.
~70% Platform Reuse Per Vertical
The ingestion pipeline, federated storage layer, schema validation, and outcomes linkage logic are shared across all verticals. Adding a new vertical requires only a device-specific parser and schema extension — typically 6–8 weeks of engineering time, not a platform rebuild.
Defensibility Layers
  • Device-physics expertise: Each parser requires deep understanding of the modality's physics, coordinate systems, and failure modes. This is not a software problem — it is a clinical-physics problem that takes years to build correctly.
  • Multi-vertical schema network effect: A registry covering 6 procedure types across oncology and surgery can answer cross-modality questions that no single-vertical database can. The combination is the moat, not any individual parser.
  • Site switching cost: Once a hospital's workflow is integrated with PROCEDA's Tier 2 edge hardware and HL7 feed, replacing the system requires a full re-integration. Churn is structurally low.
  • Regulatory lead: IRB-approved observational research protocols at 24 sites represent 18–24 months of procurement and ethics review time for any competitor entering the same institutions.

Why Each Stream Is Defensible
Site SaaS — Low Churn by Design
Switching requires removal of edge hardware, re-integration of HL7 feeds, and a new IRB amendment. Structural churn rate <5% annually. Revenue becomes annuity income once the site is live.
Pharma — 3 Active LOIs
Three major pharma programs have signed LOIs at the $180K–$750K range. The trigger condition is reaching 500 cases with 12-month follow-up in the relevant vertical. IORT hits this threshold at Month 18.
Device OEM — Aligned Incentives
Device manufacturers benefit from outcomes data showing their devices perform well. PROCEDA can offer revenue share deals where the OEM co-markets PROCEDA enrollment to their installed base, dramatically reducing PROCEDA's site acquisition cost.
Grants — Non-dilutive Runway
The SDR (sonodynamic response) signal in FUS data and the late-toxicity dataset in HDR are individually fundable as standalone research programs. These grants fund engineering time without dilution and produce publications that validate the registry's data quality.
