What PROCEDA unlocks for HDR
the dataset that has never existed at scale
HDR afterloaders produce DICOM RT objects containing every dwell position with exact 3D coordinates and per-position dwell times. Routinely stored in TPS systems — never extracted and linked to toxicity outcomes across institutions.

PROCEDA links per-dwell-position dosimetry → D2cc OAR doses → late toxicity CTCAE grades at 6/12/24 months — the first multi-institution HDR dataset correlating dose geometry with late toxicity at scale.

Why serial capture is the key insight
the data that APIs don't expose
Most RFA/MWA generators output real-time power, impedance, and temperature at 1 Hz over RS-232 serial — a protocol these devices have used for 20+ years. No network API exists. A Raspberry Pi 5 with a USB-serial adapter captures the complete physics stream continuously with no IT involvement.

The 1 Hz time-series is the signal that matters. Power-impedance-temperature curve shape predicts ablation zone geometry and completeness — a summarized API response loses this entirely. PROCEDA is the only system capturing it.

The SDR research opportunity
redefining patient selection with real-world data
Skull density ratio (SDR) predicts acoustic energy transmission through the skull. Current patient selection thresholds are based on manufacturer guidelines with limited clinical evidence.

PROCEDA links SDR + per-sonication thermometry maps to clinical outcomes across hundreds of procedures — enabling the first evidence-based SDR selection criteria for transcranial FUS. Standalone fundable grant. Landmark publication in its own right.
